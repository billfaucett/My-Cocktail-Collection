How to test

  1. Delete: swipe a cocktail and check that the alert names that drink and that only it disappears.
  2. Share: open a recipe and tap Share Recipe. The share sheet should open, even in the Simulator.
  3. Photo: add a tall or wide photo to a drink. It should keep its shape.
  4. Base spirit: after launching, filter by Gin. The Martini should appear.
  5. Delete Menu Data: long-press the menu icon and choose it. You should be asked first, and the menus list should be empty right away.
  6. Favorites: tap "Add Our Favorite Cocktails". The button should disappear.

Bug 2 — Swipe-to-delete removes the drink you swiped

Each row used to share one showAlert flag and attach its own alert, so SwiftUI could show any row's alert and delete that drink. Now the swipe stores the exact drink in drinkToDelete, and one alert on the list deletes that drink. The alert also names the drink.

Bug 3 — Sharing a recipe no longer crashes

The Messages composer crashes on devices that can't send texts, like the Simulator. It's replaced with SwiftUI's built-in ShareLink, which opens the standard share sheet (Messages, Mail, AirDrop, Notes and so on) and sends the same recipe text. MessageComposer.swift isn't used anymore, so you can delete it.

Bug 4 — Photos saved small and in proportion (+ force-unwrap cleanup)

New drinks used to throw away the resized photo and store the full-resolution image as a PNG. Edited drinks were squashed into a 300×300 square. Both paths now call cocktailPhotoData(), which shrinks the photo to at most 600px on its longest side, keeps its shape, and saves it as an 80% quality JPEG. The three ! force-unwraps are now ?? "", so a missing value can't crash the edit screen.

Bug 5 — Built-in cocktails get their base spirit (+ one DataController)

Seeding now reads the spirits straight from the database instead of an on-screen list that might not have updated yet. A new linkMissingBaseSpirits function runs at launch and links any of the 11 built-in cocktails that are missing their spirit, which repairs your existing data. Drinks you created are never changed. The helpers are now static, so the app stops creating a new database container on every call.
